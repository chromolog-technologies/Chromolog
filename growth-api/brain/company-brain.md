## How we work

1. Discovery — understand the business problem and operations.
2. Plan — scope, system design and a fixed proposal.
3. UI/UX design — prototypes the client approves.
4. Development — built in modules, with regular demos.
5. AI integration, where it adds value.
6. Testing — QA and security checks.
7. Deployment.
8. Support — maintenance, monitoring and improvements.

The first step for any serious inquiry is a **free consultation / scoping call**, after which we
send a written estimate.

## Ideal customer (who to prioritise)

- Has a clear business problem that technology can solve.
- Has a reachable decision-maker: founder, owner, director, administrator, operations manager.
- Has a realistic budget and values quality and a long-term partner over the lowest price.
- Has recurring-revenue potential: maintenance, SaaS subscription, support, further development.

Markets: **primary** India (especially Kerala); **secondary** UAE; **selective** international.

## Lead scoring guide

- **Hot** — clear need, decision-maker, budget fits our ranges, wants to start within ~3 months.
- **Warm** — real need but budget, timeline or authority is unclear.
- **Cold** — just exploring, student/research question, budget far below our ranges, or a job seeker.
